# Four directions — storyboards, 21 September 2026

Milestone 3. Each is a complete home page, built on its own branch with its own preview link. The copy is the deck's in every one; the nine colours are the palette in every one (semi-locked); everything else — type, layout, header, footer, motion — is the direction's own. Each names the predictable version it refuses, the mechanisms it borrows and from where, and the one risk it takes. All four: the wheel always moves the page; every build happens once; reduced motion shows the finished page; 60fps on a phone or it's cut.

Fonts are self-hosted, open-licensed; بداية in the two copy lines that name it falls back to Readex Pro's Arabic subset in every direction.

## A — The field

*Lando Norris's light world, toned to a firm.*

**The refused default.** Text left, a canvas on the right doing something, everything fading up.

**Type.** Onest (display, 700, tight) · IBM Plex Mono (facts: the terms, dates, the published number).

**The world.** A contour field: fine ink lines drawn live across the whole viewport, like a map of ground that hasn't been built on yet. It breathes slowly on its own; it bends gently away from the cursor on a laptop; on a phone it responds to scroll. The four colours appear only as the lines pass through a section that owns them (teal for Setup, aqua for Operations, plum for Finance, ink for Tech).

**Load.** Contours already there. The lockup and the tagline set in place; the headline's lines slide up out of a clipped box, one line every 80ms. Nothing else.

**Scroll 1 — Where you stand.** The section pins. The three columns — covered / needs building / yours to do — arrive as three long pills sliding in from the field, one per scroll step, stone with an ink hairline. The heading stays; the pills stack.

**Scroll 2 — One firm, four functions.** Pinned. The contour lines gather into the mark at centre screen (the one concept kept from the identity spec, here as a chapter rather than the opener), then the four words appear at the four compass points with their lines, and the lines beneath the mark take on the four tones.

**Scroll 3 — A few steps ahead.** Full-bleed teal panel, the title alone, huge. The contours continue through it in paper.

**Scroll 4 — How we work.** The three terms as three circles rising from below the fold (Orchid's rise), settling on a line, monospace inside.

**Scroll 5 — Before it's big.** Plain stone, the story's two owner sentences set large in italic, the rest at reading size. No motion but the entrance.

**Scroll 6 — Programs.** Aqua panel, edge to edge. Footer beneath with the watermark.

**Phone.** Same order; the pinned chapters become short pins (one screen each); the field responds to scroll instead of cursor.

**The risk.** A canvas behind body text. It stays under 12% ink and the checker measures contrast on every build.

## B — The grid

*Aspen's discipline, in Bidaya's colours, without a photo.*

**The refused default.** A centred column with rounded cards and generous whitespace.

**Type.** Geist (display, 500–700, very large — single words at 20vw) · Geist Mono (every fact, label and date; the clocks Aspen has become the published terms).

**The world.** No canvas. The grid is the world: hard-edged panels that fill the viewport edge to edge in stone, paper, teal, aqua, plum and ink. What Aspen does with halftone, Bidaya does with linear drawing: single-weight ink lines, the two circles of the mark at architectural scale, hairlines that divide.

**Load.** The header is a bar of panels: lockup · time in the UAE (mono) · nav · Start. Below, the viewport is split: left half stone with one word, *Bidaya*, at 20vw; right half teal with the mark's two circles drawn at 200% so only their arcs are visible. The word is written in with a per-letter slide; the arcs draw themselves in 900ms.

**Scroll 1.** The split flips: ink panel with the H1 in paper; stone panel with the lead. Panels slide in from opposite sides on the scroll (Aspen's sectional snap), not fade.

**Scroll 2 — Where you stand.** A three-column grid of paper panels, the column heads in mono; each panel's line count is different, so the grid is irregular on purpose.

**Scroll 3 — Four functions.** Four full-height panels in a row, each a colour, each holding one word at 12vw and its line at the bottom in mono. On a phone: four stacked screens.

**Scroll 4 — A few steps ahead.** One full-bleed teal panel, the title alone, 14vw.

**Scroll 5 — How we work.** Three panels: *Month one, no notice* / *30 days* / *In writing* — each the width of its words, the numbers in mono at 18vw.

**Scroll 6 — Before it's big.** Paper panel, the story set in two columns like a broadsheet. Programs: aqua, full width. Footer as the mirror of the header bar.

**Phone.** Everything stacks; the split hero becomes two screens; panels keep their colours.

**The risk.** Boxes. The identity spec said "boxes not". Aspen proves hard edges can be the elegance; this direction argues it and logs the decision if chosen.

## C — The method

*WeEvolveIT's scroll-through-the-process, on a light page.*

**The refused default.** A vertical page with the four functions as four cards.

**Type.** Space Grotesk (display, 700) · Space Mono (labels, the bracketed chapter names — `[ the read ]`, `[ four functions ]`, `[ the terms ]`).

**The world.** A particle stream: ink dots that travel across the page and gather into whatever the current chapter is about — the number four, the mark, three circles — then loosen and travel on. The stream is the connective tissue; the chapters are the slides.

**Load.** Stone, the lockup, the bracketed label `[ the executive layer ]`, the H1 with the stream passing behind it from the top right. The headline's words arrive on the stream.

**Scroll 1 — Where you stand.** The page stops scrolling down and starts scrolling sideways: three slides — covered / needs building / yours to do — each a full viewport, the stream running along the top, the label incrementing `01 / 03`. (Founder: "that's what I really liked".)

**Scroll 2 — One firm, four functions.** The same sideways move: Setup · Operations · Finance · Tech as four full-width slides, each with its colour as a band across the lower third, the stream forming each function's word out of dots before the paragraph fades in beneath.

**Scroll 3 — A few steps ahead.** Vertical again: a teal panel with the title alone. The stream crosses it in paper.

**Scroll 4 — How we work.** Sideways for the third and last time: three terms, three slides, each with its short line at 10vw and its sentence in mono beneath.

**Scroll 5 — Before it's big.** Vertical, calm, the story at reading size with the two owner sentences in italic.

**Programs.** Aqua panel. A scroll-progress mark in the corner throughout (mono, `042%`), as on WeEvolveIT.

**Phone.** The sideways chapters become swipeable slides with the same progress mark; the vertical page continues underneath.

**The risk.** Three horizontal chapters is a lot of gesture. Each must feel like arriving somewhere, not like a carousel; if the third one doesn't, it's cut to two.

## D — The synthesis

*My call: the parts of the six that belong to Bidaya, in one page.*

**The refused default.** Any of the above done timidly.

**Why these parts.** Aspen's sectional rhythm — every section a different shape, the page full without being filled — is the spine, because the founder named it as the feel. WeEvolveIT's sideways chapter is used exactly once, for the four functions, because a firm with four scopes has the one thing that deserves a slideshow. Orchid's rising pills carry the three terms, because terms are the proof and should arrive like evidence. Filmbot's varied text entrances give each section a different way in, so nothing repeats. Lando's field lives only in the hero, toned to a firm. Rapidkert's card fan carries Programs, because a partnership is a stack of things held together.

**Type.** Bricolage Grotesque (display, 700–800; it has a voice at large sizes and disappears into good manners at small) · IBM Plex Mono (the facts layer) · body in Bricolage at 400.

**The world.** Stone. Ink lines are the drawing tool everywhere: hairlines, arcs of the mark, the contour field in the hero only.

**Load.** Full viewport. The lockup top left; the header is a single hairline with the nav on it. The contour field is already breathing in the lower two thirds. The tagline is set; the H1 arrives one line at a time, each line sliding up from under a hairline that draws itself first (Filmbot). The two buttons are pills that draw their outline before filling.

**Scroll 1 — Where you stand.** The field fades. A full-bleed paper panel; the section title at 8vw on the left third; on the right two-thirds, the three column heads in mono, and beneath each, the deck's line — the three columns rising into place one scroll-step apart, as pills (Orchid). Beneath, *The read* in mono.

**Scroll 2 — One firm, four functions.** The page turns sideways: four slides — Setup (teal), Operations (aqua), Finance (plum), Tech (ink) — each full-bleed in its colour with paper type, the function word at 14vw, its sentence beneath, a `1 / 4` mark in mono. The mark, in paper, is drawn one arc per slide so that on the fourth it is complete (the four-tone build, reinterpreted). Then the page returns to vertical with "Where a need falls outside these four…" on stone.

**Scroll 3 — A few steps ahead.** Teal, edge to edge, the title alone at 12vw, the line beneath on stone. The entrance: the panel opens from a hairline in the middle, like a letter (Filmbot).

**Scroll 4 — How we work.** The three terms as three circles of different sizes (236 / 180 / 300), rising from below the fold at different speeds and settling; inside each, the term in mono and its sentence. The heading's sentence — *Terms a firm that couldn't deliver would never publish* — at 6vw above them.

**Scroll 5 — Before it's big.** Stone, quiet. The two owner sentences in italic at 4vw; the rest at reading width. One entrance: the italic lines slide in from the left as the reader reaches them.

**Scroll 6 — Programs.** Aqua. Three paper cards — *For cultural and educational institutions* / *and the practitioners they host* / *Built as a partnership* — fanned in shallow 3D (Rapidkert), squaring up as the panel is reached.

**Footer.** The mark alone as the watermark, the seven links in mono, the two lines.

**Header.** Stays put (the founder doesn't mind it): a hairline with the lockup and the nav, stone at 92% over the page.

**Phone.** The hero keeps the field; the sideways chapter becomes four swipe slides; the circles stack; the fan becomes a stack.

**The risk.** Six different section shapes on one page. Aspen shows it holds when the type system is strict; the mono layer and the one display face are what hold it.
