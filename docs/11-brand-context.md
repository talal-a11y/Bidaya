## Brand

- **Name:** Bidaya — بداية, Arabic for *beginning*. The organising idea; on the homepage as the name block (*بداية means beginning. A business is decided at its start.*).
- **Category noun (machine-readable blocks only, never a headline):** management and tech consultancy, UAE.
- **Position:** *Bidaya is the executive layer for UAE businesses that don't have one yet — one firm running setup, operations, finance and tech from the beginning, on terms it publishes.* (Lock §10 v2; ledger AD, AE.)
- **Tagline:** *Executive management, from the beginning.* **Hero:** *Setup, operations, finance and tech — run inside your business by one firm.* (Phase 5 lockup test passed; Phase 9 usage search outstanding.)
- **Identity (Phase 5, locked):** the existing mark kept exactly; mirrored bilingual lockup primary; light and cool — stone, paper, ink, aqua, teal, plum; Readex Pro for both scripts; ribboned sections; the flow that becomes, twice. Identity system spec v1.0; guidelines v1.0; logo package 21 September.
- **Stage:** operating, founder-led with a bench of freelancers and licensed partner firms; assistant hire imminent; pre-launch on the new identity and site. Founded by Talal Abdel-Hamid — named once, deep in the site, no CV, no year (decision 6).
- **Vocabulary that is law:** "the executive layer" in every sentence of copy; "executive management" only in the tagline and role-naming headlines; never "executive support" / "executive decisions"; "tech," never "technology"; the four functions always *setup, operations, finance, tech*; "founders and owners"; "done right" or "properly"; "fractional" on intent pages, FAQ and metadata only.

**Engagement shapes:** monthly, rolling (30-day notice anytime); monthly, term-based for deadline-driven work (6/12 months, exitable on 30 days); fixed-scope projects at milestones. Trial month at full rate, notice waived. Flat pricing; no setup fee. Discovery is phase one of every engagement. (Addendum v2.)

**Executive seats:** CFO, COO, CEO. No fractional CTO; tech is delivered inside every engagement and as fixed-scope technical projects (ledger K).

**Deliberately not doing:** marketing, design and PR as sold services; one-off brand projects outside a retainer; standalone formation/PRO work; anything touching client funds; unpaid discovery beyond the 30-minute call.

## Audience

Three audiences, one register (ledger H). The primary converts from the site, the secondary chooses a shape on it, the tertiary verifies through it.

- **Rania, 32** — founder, seed-funded, Dubai; monthly engagement. Decides on mechanisms.
- **Khalid, 41** — owner, twelve-year-old contracting business, Sharjah; one-time first. The owner the site must read to; resents "startup."
- **Noor, 36** — head of programs, cultural foundation; Programs only; never sees a price.

**Reconciliation:** the buyer wants speed of execution, not speed of commitment; "aspiring" routes to Programs; "hustle" is never copy. **Frame (ledger AA):** the owner is stretched, never incapable; the business is described by stage, never by size or means (AD).

**Their language:** *I have an idea and no idea where to begin. It's getting too big to handle on my own. Too much on my plate. Done right. A few steps ahead of me. Stay on the business.*

**Failure states 5 and 6** — unprofitable without knowing why; fines from missed obligations — carry a number and are never softened.

## Proof strategy

No client names, logos, testimonials, "trusted by," "proven track record," or founder credentials. Ranked (lock §11 v2):

1. ***Where you stand*** — the ungated read: where you stand / what is due (with AED penalties) / what to do first / the honest line. Spec v1.2. The only demonstrated proof at launch and the effective launch gate. Incentive layer: *Send me this and the files* — the method templates and register updates, by email or WhatsApp.
2. The trial month, notice waived. 3. Thirty-day exit on every shape. 4. The published floor — monthly from AED 12,000, the only number on the site. 5. The written do-it-yourself outcome. 6. Anonymised engagements — month-one-from-zero, the marine consultancy, ESK after delivery — **wording drafted by assumption in the Phase 7 copy deck; past tense only; founder confirmation outstanding** (F, X, AT). 7. Programs. 8. Reports and notes — one a quarter and one a month; the umbrella name assumed as *Reports and notes* (AU); authorship open (V).

**The weakest link, on the record:** at launch nothing on the site shows delivery except item 6, whose wording is unapproved. The tool and the mechanisms carry the load.

## Point of view

Primary enemy: build-then-find-out. Secondary: hiring a consultancy too late. The cost that qualifies the position: losing the founder who wants to move without looking, and turning down work that isn't ready. Manifesto v1 (Phase 4) is the full expression; quiet conviction, internal use.

**Vision (2–4 years; ledger Q):** a hub and platform for founders, creatives and young businesses — stated direction, endorsed architecture, own name when it exists. **Guardrail: no hub / platform / space language on the site at launch.**

## Programs

A partnership structure co-delivered with institutions and businesses, whose beneficiaries are creative businesses and independent practitioners. Co-funded (Bidaya + sponsors + partners); mission and proof for now, outside the AED 60K MRR goal; co-branded or Bidaya-branded-and-hosted as the goal, partner-branded with credit early. Never fractional through another door; never priced; institutional register. Core message: *Business support inside the programs you already run, co-delivered.* (Lock §12; ledger O.)

## Constraints

1. **§8 is law**, checked before anything is presented; ledger M is the proposed append. The escape clause covers §9 only, by asking first.
2. **§9 visual, locked as amended (AI):** rounded corners; light primary — warm stone, never pure white — with dark carried by ink and the coloured bands; says what it does immediately; motion below Plaid; rigorous responsive; semantic HTML for AI readers. Excluded: orange primary, BCG green, pure white dominant, emoji, the Inter/Poppins/Montserrat/Open Sans stack. Category imagery rejected wholesale (N): no skyline, boardroom, handshake, executive portraiture. Register: advanced, not young.
3. **Copy boundaries:** no prices outside How we work, intent pages and FAQ; nothing on Programs; no future-tense delivery promises; no emirate, no office; no hub/platform; no founder credentials.
4. **Bilingual-ready build**; Arabic copy by a native professional; the bilingual wordmark tested across three lockups plus the existing mark (decision 8).
5. **GEO:** priced, FAQ-structured, dated, schema-marked intent pages; an llms.txt; category query terms on intent pages only (P3, P4).
