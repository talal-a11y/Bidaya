# Bidaya — Brand Guidelines

**Phase 6 output, v1.0 — 21 September 2026.** Produced by `brand-guidelines-builder` from the strategy lock (v2.1), the voice rulebook (v1.5), the messaging framework (v1.4), the brand story (v2.4), the manifesto (v1), the identity system spec (v1.0) and Part 1 (v2), under ledger v15.

**How this document works.** One source per subject. The rulebook owns the voice; the messaging framework owns the messages; the identity system spec owns the visual rules; the lock owns the position. This guide carries what a person needs at hand on a working day and points to the owning document for the rest — it copies nothing it could reference, so no rule exists in two places to drift apart. Where the skill's template asked for something the record has ruled out, the template is overridden and the override is stated.

**Overrides of the template, stated:** no brand personality words (decision 11 — the voice is described by what it does); no CMYK or Pantone conversions (none exist until a print application is ordered; a printer's proof decides); no social post templates (no channel plan exists); no emoji anywhere (§9).

## Part 1 — Foundation

### About this guide

For anyone who writes, designs or builds for Bidaya — a developer at Phase 8, a writer at Phase 7, a future hire, a Programs partner's designer placing the mark beside their own. Read the part you need. Every rule here has a test or a number; if a rule seems to need interpretation, the owning document has the reasoning.

### What Bidaya is

**Position — one sentence.** *Bidaya is the executive layer for UAE businesses that don't have one yet — one firm running setup, operations, finance and tech from the beginning, on terms it publishes.*

**Framework.** For founders and owners of funded startups and trading SMEs in the UAE, Bidaya is the executive layer before they can hire one — one firm operating at CEO, CFO and COO scope across setup, operations, finance and tech — because it runs the business rather than advising it, and publishes the terms that prove it. *(Lock §10, quoted as locked; see the audit, issue 1, on its punctuation.)*

**Mission**, provisional wording with the substance locked (lock §03): *Bidaya runs the parts of a business its owner cannot yet hire for, so the business is built properly from the start and the owner's time goes to the business itself — a few steps ahead, and treating every business it works inside as its own.*

**Vision** — founder-stated, horizon 2–4 years (lock §02): the place where the UAE's founders, creatives and young businesses learn from one another and get their businesses run properly. **Binding guardrail:** none of it appears on the site at launch — no hub, platform or space language anywhere.

**Values** (lock §04) — four, each a stance rather than an adjective:
- **As our own.** The businesses Bidaya works inside are treated as its own — an executive's stance, not a vendor's.
- **Properly, from the beginning.** The study before the recommendation; the structural step now rather than paid for later.
- **Ahead.** The next filing, the next hire, the next requirement, surfaced before the owner asks.
- **Whole.** The business, not the function — the executive layer across all of it, and the network managed for the rest.

**Personality.** None stated, by decision 11. What the brand *does* stands in for what it *is*: it names the thing, states the number, gives the decision back, says no plainly, and proves by mechanism rather than adjective. *(Rulebook §01, §03.)*

**The name.** بداية means beginning. A business is decided at its start. The name-meaning block lives on the homepage, not in About as etymology.

**Three audiences, one register** (lock §06): the founder of a funded company (monthly engagement); the owner of an established business (one-time first); the head of programs at a cultural institution (Programs — never sees a price). "Founders and owners," never "founders" alone. The site reads to all three; it pitches to none of them as a startup.

**What Bidaya refuses to be** (lock §14): not for the business that wants to move without looking; never competing on price; does not sell seats; claims no exclusivity mechanism; not a marketing, design or PR agency; Programs is not services through another door; the founder is not the brand.

## Part 2 — Visual identity

*Owner: the identity system spec v1.0. The rules below are the ones needed daily; the spec has the geometry, the contrast table and the open items.*

### The mark

An eccentric ring — a hilal, the bowl of the ب. Two circles, evenodd; the founder's existing mark, kept exactly. **Whole or not at all:** never cropped, halved, rotated, mirrored, outlined, patterned, shadowed, used as an icon, or standing in for the B in Bidaya. Never yellow, saffron or green.

**Five states, and no sixth:** ink on light; paper on the coloured bands; the small variant below 32px; the four-tone mark — motion only, the end of the four-functions build, never a still, never the logo; the watermark at 9%, bottom-right, once per page.

### The lockups — decision 8, closed

| Use | Lockup |
|---|---|
| Default, wherever there is room | **Mirrored** — بداية · mark · Bidaya, each script reading outward from the mark |
| Anywhere | **The mark alone** |
| The site header only | **One line with a hairline** between Bidaya and بداية. Without the hairline this arrangement is rejected on every surface |
| Proposals and their covers | **Stacked** — Bidaya over بداية |
| Programs, Arabic-first partners | Arabic lead — reserved, unruled |

Wordmark: Readex Pro 700, sentence case, tracking −2%. Mark height 1.75 × the wordmark's size. Clear space: one inner radius on all sides. Minimum size, pure form: 32px on screen, 10mm in print. Files: the logo package of 21 September — use them; do not reset the wordmark from a font.

### Colour

| Token | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| stone | #F4F1EC | The page. Pure white is never a page |
| paper | #FCFBF9 | Cards, reading surfaces |
| ink | #241B2E | Text, the mark |
| muted | #6A5F78 | Secondary text on light |
| aqua | #0B6F7A | The accent: actions, emphasis, the aqua band, the small-mark field |
| teal | #2A8F99 | Band; titles only on it |
| plum | #5A47A3 | Band |
| soft | #DCD6CE | Hairlines, borders |

**Rules.** No gradients anywhere, ever. Teal and plum are bands, never text on light. Text on the bands is paper; the teal band carries large text only (body text fails AA on teal in every colour — Part 1 §2, open question 1). The mark is ink on light, paper on bands. No new colour without a token proposal on the ledger. **Print:** no CMYK or Pantone values exist; a printer's proof decides when a print piece is ordered.

### Typography — decision 9, closed

**Readex Pro**, one family for both scripts: display 700, body 400, labels and emphasis 600, the Arabic wordmark 600. Open licence; self-hosted at build. Fallback: `system-ui, -apple-system, "Segoe UI", sans-serif`.

| Level | Weight | Size | Use |
|---|---|---|---|
| Display 1 | 700 | 34–68px, 15ch max | Hero |
| Display 2 | 700 | 28–44px, 20ch max | Section titles |
| H1 / H2 / H3 | 700 / 600 / 600 | 30–48 / 24–34 / 18px | Page and section structure |
| Body | 400 | 16px, line height 1.55, 66ch max | Default |
| Lead | 400 | 18px | First paragraphs |
| Caption | 400 | 14px | Legal line, captions |
| Label | 600 | 13px | Chips, small buttons |

Sentence case everywhere. No title case, no letter-spaced small caps, no overline style. Numerals for money and time: AED 12,000; 30 days. Body never bolder than 600. One family per page. *(Scale provisional until Phase 8.)*

### Imagery, icons, motion, shapes

- **No photography at launch; no stock or generated imagery ever.** The category's props (skylines, boardrooms, handshakes, rising arrows, compass and rocket icons, gradient heroes) are banned by §8.
- **The flow** is the only visual language: a background presence in the hero, and two moments where it gathers and becomes something solid — the logo, once, and the four-functions mark, one function at a time. Everything else arrives with a quiet reveal. Every build happens once and stays. Visuals never explain content; not every section has one.
- **Icons:** outline, 1.75px on a 24px grid, Phosphor. Proposed — "okay, could be better."
- **Shapes:** sections meet on ribbons, never straight edges; pill cards, circles, blobs; radii 8 · 12 · 20 · 28 · 48 · pill. Bouba, not Kiki.
- **Reduced motion:** everything appears in place.

## Part 3 — Verbal identity

*Owner: the voice rulebook v1.5 — fifteen rules with tests, the vocabulary, the never-say lists, the pre-ship checklist. Its extraction half (the founder's own cadence) waits on the voice note. Nothing below replaces it.*

**Voice essence.** *Bidaya sounds like the senior person in the room who has done the work before, writing to someone who is doing too much of it alone.*

**Tone, in one line each.** Formal-leaning, not stiff (contractions yes; slang, emoji, exclamation never). Serious, not generic — seriousness carried by specifics, not adjectives. Authoritative by evidence, accessible by construction. Calm — bold in position, calm in delivery. Warm through attention, never affection.

**The fifteen rules, by name** — each has a test in the rulebook: 1 name the thing · 2 state the number · 3 the owner is stretched, never incapable · 4 give the decision back · 5 decision first, reason second · 6 say no plainly · 7 mechanism, not adjective · 8 past tense conditionally, future tense never · 9 one "X, not Y" per page · 10 plural always · 11 headlines say what the page does · 12 address the owner, not the founder · 13 category words live on intent pages · 14 Programs is a partnership, never a service · 15 stage, not size.

**Writing style.** Short sentences by default; one long one per paragraph when it carries specifics; never three fragments in a row. Full stops and commas do the work; at most one em dash per paragraph, never two in a sentence, never a three-part rhythm. Sentence case. Numerals for money and time; "AED 12,000," never "12K" or "Dhs." Contractions yes, except in terms and legal lines. Active voice. Dry understatement is the only humour.

**We say:** the executive layer (in copy) · executive management (the tagline and role-naming headlines only) · setup, operations, finance and tech — in that order, always · tech, never technology · run, take, hold, build, hand back · the owner; founders and owners · a few steps ahead (sparingly) · done right; properly · on the tools you already use · runs with or without us · in writing · the study · clients · monthly or fixed-scope · partners, practitioners, institutions, programs.

**We don't say:** under one roof · one-stop shop · all your business needs · at a fraction of the cost · cannot afford; small · without the overhead · strategic partner; trusted partner · tailored; bespoke; seamless; end-to-end; holistic · leverage; elevate; empower; streamline; unlock; transform; robust; navigate · journey; passionate; world-class; solutions · affordable · hub; platform · assessment; scorecard; readiness · the only; in-house; under one license · begin right; start right; the right beginning · services or a price on the Programs path · Bidaya Consulting; Bidaya LLC in copy · a client's name · the founder's credentials · the emirate; the office.

Before anything ships, the rulebook's thirteen-item checklist runs on it. It is mechanical, searches and counts, and it is not optional.

## Part 4 — Messaging

*Owner: the messaging framework v1.4.*

**Core message.** *Bidaya helps founders and owners of UAE businesses run properly before they can hire an executive team, by acting as their executive layer across setup, operations, finance and tech — monthly or for one defined piece of work — on terms it publishes.*

**Value proposition, spoken in ten seconds.** *We're the executive layer for businesses that don't have one yet. We run the operating side — setup through tech — so the owner doesn't have to.*

**Tagline.** ***Executive management, from the beginning.*** With the mark on the kit cover, the title slide and the homepage; never in a page title; never repeated by the hero. Usage search still due at Phase 9.

**Hero headline.** ***Setup, operations, finance and tech — run inside your business by one firm.***

**Thesis line.** *بداية means beginning. A business is decided at its start.*

**Four key messages, with their proof:** 1 one firm runs the whole operating side (four functions in one engagement; licensed specialists in the fee; your tools, not new ones) · 2 judgment before the sale (*Where you stand*: the read in full, no email; what is due with the cost of missing it; the honest closing line) · 3 terms a firm that couldn't deliver would never publish (first month at full rate with no notice; thirty-day exit; paid discovery beyond the first call; "you don't need us" in writing) · 4 handed back in a form that runs without us.

**Placement rules that are not negotiable.** The only number on the site is *monthly from AED 12,000*, and it appears on How we work, intent pages and FAQ only — never the hero, About, the story or Programs. Project work is quoted after the call. "Fractional" lives on intent pages, FAQ and metadata only. Programs has its own core message and no price. "A few steps ahead" describes Bidaya relative to the owner, never to rivals. No promise of a first-month outcome. No client names; anonymised engagements only with approved wording, in the past tense.

## Part 5 — Brand in use

*Owner: the identity system spec §1 (applications) and the In use tab of the identity board.*

### Digital

- **Website header:** the one-line lockup with the hairline — mark 36px, wordmark 21px, the Arabic 18px in muted; on phones the mark and wordmark only. Primary button: aqua pill, paper text. Ghost button: transparent, ink border. Sections on stone and paper with ribboned aqua, teal and plum bands. The hero builds the logo once, top-left; the header's relation to it on scroll is a Phase 8 decision.
- **Page titles:** under 30 characters: *Bidaya*; *Where you stand — Bidaya*; *How we work — Bidaya*. Never the tagline. (Home title tag: recommended exception *Bidaya — executive management*; Phase 8.)
- **Favicon, app icon, avatar:** the rounded aqua field with the paper mark at 62% — one family of small marks, one colour. Files in the package.
- **Company profiles (LinkedIn, directories):** the avatar above; the value proposition's first two sentences; no price; no tagline in the name field.
- **Email:** signature is the mark at 32px and four lines — name, role and firm, the descriptor line *Setup · Operations · Finance · Tech*. No banner, no image, no tagline. Subject lines decision-first. One ask per email.
- **The tool:** second person; the read as three columns of judgment; no score, percentage, arrow or "readiness." Spec: bidaya-tool-spec.md v1.3.

### Print and documents

- **Business card:** front, the mark alone centred on stone; back, the header lockup with the descriptor line. Print is light — cards and PDFs.
- **Proposals:** the stacked lockup top-left of the cover; the mark and name in page headers, page number in the foot, watermark bottom-right at 9%. Tiers by scope inside; never on the site. The founder's personal note is his.
- **Reports and Notes:** report covers carry the mark alone with an aqua ribbon at the foot; every figure has a named source and a date. One report a quarter, one note a month.
- **Presentations:** stone slides; title slide with the mark alone top-left and the title low; aqua openers with the paper mark; Readex Pro at the scale above; no icon grids, no stat triads.
- **Programs, beside a partner's mark:** Bidaya's mark in single colour at the partner's visual height, a hairline between, host first. Never Bidaya's colour next to a partner's colour. *(Proposed; unruled.)*

### Brand don'ts — the short list

- [ ] Never crop, rotate, outline, recolour or pattern the mark; never below 32px without the small variant.
- [ ] Never a gradient, a photograph, stock or generated imagery.
- [ ] Never a colour outside the nine tokens; never body text on the teal band; never pure white as a page.
- [ ] Never a typeface other than Readex Pro; never title case or letter-spaced caps.
- [ ] Never the one-line lockup without its hairline; never the four-tone mark as a still or as the logo.
- [ ] Never "the only," "in-house," "under one license," "one roof," "fractional" outside intent pages, or any phrase on the rulebook's §08.
- [ ] Never a price outside How we work, intent pages and FAQ; never a price or "services" on Programs; never a client's name; never the founder's credentials; never the emirate or the office.
- [ ] Never describe the owner as lacking ability, or the business by its size or what it can't afford.
- [ ] Never promise what a first month will deliver.
- [ ] Never ship without the rulebook's checklist.
