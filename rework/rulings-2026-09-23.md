Where the moved lines land — one unlinked page per floor, or one page carrying all three — is Claude Code's call, logged; the pages are excluded from the navigation and the sitemap but not from search engines or AI crawlers, since being found is their purpose. The creatives floor and the founders' project floor have no line written yet; their wording comes with the copy deck v2 — until then, the monthly floor lines are the only ones to move.
