## The executive layer, run inside your business

### What we do

We operate at a CEO, CFO, and COO scope across setup, operations, finance, and tech, offering fractional executive services at a fraction of the full time cost, allowing young businesses and SMEs to operate with confidence without the need for a full-time corporate hire. The owner keeps every decision that counts. We prepare the ground so those decisions are made on time, with ease, and with confidence.

### How we do it

@rows
**Monthly.** The executive layer inside the business on an ongoing basis. Rolling, on thirty days' notice, or on a term matched to a deadline. This is the shape most engagements take once the foundations exist, and remains anywhere from 3 months to 24 months. Our aim is not to extend our stay, it is to build the business sustainably for it to run without our direct intervention, and hand it back to the founders and their new team.

**One defined piece of work.** A fixed scope, a fixed fee, a beginning and an end. A study of how the business actually runs. A structure built and handed over. A technical build. This is the shape most engagements take at their early stages to support with pre and post launch structuring and operations.
@end

Neither is the lesser version of the other. Which shape fits is what the first call decides.
