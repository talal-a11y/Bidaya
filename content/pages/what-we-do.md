# The executive layer, run inside your business

@lead A funded company or an established business reaches a point where the operating side is too much for one person and too early for three senior hires. Bidaya fills that layer. We operate at CEO, CFO and COO scope across setup, operations, finance and tech, inside the business, reporting to the owner. The owner keeps every decision that counts. We prepare the ground so those decisions are made on numbers, earlier, with less on the owner's desk.

---

## What the role covers

@note four rows, plain, no grid of icons.

@rows
**Setup.** Entity structure and the licensing decision. Formation coordinated with the specialists it needs. The foundations decided once, so they don't have to be redone at the first hire, the first investor or the first audit.

**Operations.** How the business runs when the owner isn't in the room: process written down, the people plan, the hiring roadmap and the hires themselves, the review rhythm that keeps the month on track.

**Finance.** Management reporting every month, budgeting and cash flow, the reporting an investor or a bank asks for, and the filings that carry a fine when they're missed. Licensed tax consultants and accountants are included in the fee.

**Tech.** The systems under all of it: the accounting stack, the records, the automation that removes a job rather than adds a tool. Built on what the business already uses. One-time technical projects, from a website to a custom internal system, are in scope on their own.
@end

Where a need sits outside these four, we know who does it and we manage them. The client has one firm to call.

---

## Two shapes of the same role

@rows
**Monthly.** The executive layer inside the business on an ongoing basis. Rolling, on thirty days' notice, or on a term matched to a deadline. This is the shape most engagements take once the foundations exist.

**One defined piece of work.** A fixed scope, a fixed fee, a beginning and an end. A study of how the business actually runs. A structure built and handed over. A technical build. Discovery is the first phase of every one, inside the fee, and the work is handed back in a form that runs without us.
@end

Neither is the lesser version of the other. Which shape fits is what the first call decides, and *Where you stand* will usually have told you before the call.
