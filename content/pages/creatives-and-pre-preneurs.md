## How We Do It

@rows
**Workshops, Trainings, & Ongoing Advisory.** Bidaya aims to continue its training and advisory in person and online. Be on the lookout for our trainings and workshops across the UAE, aiming to teach better business acumen to young aspiring entrepreneurs and provide the tools required to build right from the start.

**Programs and Partnerships.** Bidaya also aims to partner with educational and creative institutions to create events, competitions, and programs that teach, build, and support aspiring entrepreneurs early in their journey.

**Community Support.** Bidaya aims to provide better opportunities for smaller and younger businesses to compete by creating a collective of small businesses that grow and support one another. Through the network and community we have built, SMEs can benefit from lower rates that would instead be hard to get for a single entity. One small business may not afford the minimum orders or high rates, but a collective of businesses lowers overheads through sharing orders, spaces, and resources among one another. Bidaya hopes to manage a collective to support lowering the costs and the barriers of entry for young businesses.

## Inside the Programs Institutions Already Run

Residencies, cohorts, incubators and competitions teach craft well. What their practitioners are rarely taught is the company: licensing, pricing, invoicing, the numbers, the first hire. Programs is where Bidaya does that work, inside the structures institutions already run, as a partner in them.
